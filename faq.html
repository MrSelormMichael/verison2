Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about menopause care, hormone therapy, and telehealth at my hotflash MD.

What is menopause and when does it start?

Menopause is defined as the time when you've gone 12 consecutive months without a menstrual period. For most women, this occurs between ages 45 and 55, with the average age being 51. Perimenopause — the transitional period leading up to menopause — can begin 8-10 years earlier and bring many of the symptoms associated with this life stage.

What symptoms can I expect during perimenopause and menopause?

Common symptoms include hot flashes, night sweats, sleep disruption, mood changes, brain fog, vaginal dryness, decreased libido, weight changes, and joint pain. Every woman's experience is unique — some may have mild symptoms while others experience more significant challenges.

Is hormone therapy (HRT) safe?

For many women, hormone therapy is safe and effective when prescribed appropriately. Current guidelines support HRT as the most effective treatment for managing menopause symptoms. Your provider will assess your individual risk factors and help you make an informed decision. We recommend starting HRT within 10 years of menopause onset for the best risk-benefit profile.

What types of hormone therapy are available?

Options include estrogen alone (for those who've had a hysterectomy) or combined estrogen-progestogen therapy. Available delivery methods include oral pills, transdermal patches, gels, sprays, and vaginal preparations. Your provider will help determine the best formulation based on your symptoms, health history, and preferences.

How does telehealth work at my hotflash MD?

Our visits are conducted via secure video conferencing from the comfort of your home. During your appointment, we'll discuss your symptoms, medical history, and goals. Prescriptions are sent directly to your pharmacy, and you can reach out with questions between visits through our secure messaging system.

Do you accept insurance?

We are an out-of-network provider. We provide detailed superbills that you can submit to your insurance for potential reimbursement. We accept all major credit cards and HSA/FSA cards. Contact us for more information about pricing and payment options.

How often will I need to have visits?

Initial consultations are comprehensive and typically last 45-60 minutes. Follow-up visits are scheduled based on your individual needs — usually every 1-3 months initially, then less frequently as your plan stabilizes. We also offer check-ins between scheduled appointments as needed.
